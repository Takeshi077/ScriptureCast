How It Works

Four simple steps to get scripture on screen.

1

Open the Dashboard

Open the operator dashboard on your laptop. Enable speech recognition or type verses manually.

2

Connect Your Projector

Identify your displays and open the projector screen. Drag it to the HDMI output or let the desktop app handle it automatically.

3

Preach Naturally

As you speak, ScriptureCast transcribes in real-time and detects Bible references automatically.

4

Verse Appears on Screen

High-confidence matches display instantly on the projector. You can review or confirm lower-confidence detections.
