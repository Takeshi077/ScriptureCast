How It Works

Four steps to scripture on screen.

1

Open the Dashboard

Launch the operator dashboard on your laptop. Enable speech recognition or type verses manually.

2

Connect Your Projector

Identify displays and open the projector screen. Drag to HDMI or let the desktop app handle it.

3

Preach Naturally

Speak as you normally would. ScriptureCast transcribes in real-time and detects references.

4

Verse Appears on Screen

High-confidence matches display instantly. Review or confirm lower-confidence detections.
